Privacy Policy

App: Accounts Orbit (package com.bolkarigar.app)
Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Accounts Orbit Android app on Google Play, the website at accountsorbit.com, and the web app at app.accountsorbit.com. It explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and how you can delete it.

1. Who we are

Accounts Orbit is shop billing and accounts software for business owners in India. Contact: support@accountsorbit.com or support@bolkarigar.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account: name, email, username, and a hashed password when you sign up.
  • Business records you enter: invoices, parties, stock, payments, receipts, notes, staff names you add, and company profile (name, address, optional GSTIN, optional UPI ID, phone).
  • Photos you choose: product or visiting-card images you pick with the system photo picker. We do not have ongoing access to your full gallery.
  • Microphone: only while you use Voice billing, and only after you allow it.
  • Camera: only if you take a photo inside the app, and only after you allow it.
  • Device: basic technical logs such as app errors. We do not collect contacts, location, or SMS.
  • Payments: plan payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store full card numbers.

3. How we use this information

  • To create and log in to your shop account.
  • To sync invoices, ledgers, stock and staff data you save.
  • To send OTP, password reset and account emails.
  • To process Pro / Business subscription payments.
  • To provide optional voice and AI help when you use those features.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your shop data for third-party advertising.

4. Permissions

  • Internet — to save and load your account.
  • Microphone — optional voice billing.
  • Camera — optional photo capture.
  • Photos are selected through the Android system picker. The app does not request all-files or full photo-library access.

You can turn a permission off in Android Settings → Apps → Accounts Orbit → Permissions.

5. Sharing with service providers

  • Cloud hosting (application and database).
  • Razorpay for payments.
  • Email delivery for OTP and account mail.
  • Optional AI providers only when you use Voice / AI assistant.

Staff accounts you invite can see the shop data you choose to share with their role. They do not pay separately.

6. Storage and security

Data is stored on cloud servers and sent over HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed. You are responsible for keeping your login private and for data you enter about your customers.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your account while it is active. To delete your account and associated shop data, email support@accountsorbit.com from the registered email. We will delete the account within a reasonable period unless law requires us to keep a record.

8. Children

Accounts Orbit is for business use. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account data by emailing us. You may also stop using the app and uninstall it at any time.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
